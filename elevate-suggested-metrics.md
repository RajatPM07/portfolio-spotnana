# Elevate and recommendation engine, suggested additions

**All items below are SUGGESTED AND UNVERIFIED. None of them appear in the portfolio. Confirm any that are true and I will fold them into the copy, reject the rest.**

The live portfolio copy uses only the confirmed facts you provided. These are the metrics and angles a hiring manager reviewing a recommendation engine and market share story would typically probe next.

## For the recommendation engine

1. **Experiment design.** Were the 2.5 percent retention lift and the 5.3x acceptance gain measured against a holdout or control group? "Measured against a randomized holdout" is a big credibility upgrade if true.
2. **Model quality beyond AUC-ROC.** Precision or recall at the operating threshold, or a calibration statement. AUC-ROC alone is a screening metric, the threshold choice is the product decision.
3. **Coverage.** What percentage of the portfolio receives a scored next best action? "At portfolio scale" is stronger with a number.
4. **Scoring cadence and freshness.** Batch or real time, and how often the model is retrained or monitored for drift. This pairs well with the "production guardrails" claim.
5. **Incremental revenue attribution.** The rupee value of cross sell GWP or revenue attributable to the engine, paired with the percentage lift, matching the guidance to pair absolutes with baselines.
6. **What the guardrails actually were.** Offer frequency caps, suppression rules for recent claimants, eligibility filters. Even one concrete example makes "production guardrails" real.

## For Elevate

7. **Persistency or renewal rate movement.** Share growth is most impressive if retention quality held or improved alongside it.
8. **Loss ratio or combined ratio.** Evidence the share gain was not bought with underwriting deterioration. Even "loss ratio held flat" is a strong senior signal.
9. **Distribution mix.** Which channels drove the share gain, and whether any new segment or product variant from the PRDs was launched and at what adoption.
10. **The APM mentorship outcome.** What the Associate PM independently owns now. One concrete sentence turns a mentorship claim into evidence.
